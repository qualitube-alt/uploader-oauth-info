QualiTube Uploader

A desktop tool for uploading and scheduling videos on YouTube channels that the operator owns or is authorised to manage.

What the app does

  • Uploads selected local videos immediately or with a scheduled publication time.
  • Reads channel information and uploaded-video status to show reservations and avoid duplicate uploads.
  • Updates video metadata and, when explicitly configured by the operator, deletes videos confirmed as blocked.
  • Uses the operator’s Multilogin browser profile for Google authorisation and network requests.

Personal OAuth trial

This site documents the owner-operated OAuth trial of QualiTube Uploader. It does not provide a public sign-in service or collect Google credentials through this website. The trial uses the existing desktop app and the operator’s own channel.

Optional AI metadata

If enabled, selected video content is sent to Google Gemini with the operator’s API key to generate titles, descriptions or tags. This is separate from YouTube channel authorisation.

Permissions and control

You choose the Google account and channel during authorisation. You can revoke the app’s Google access at any time. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use before granting access.

Privacy questions: contact the maintainer through GitHub Issues. Do not post passwords, tokens, private videos, or other confidential information in a public issue.
